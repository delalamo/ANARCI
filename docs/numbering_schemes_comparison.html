Scheme Characteristics
Scheme Total Positions Insertion Strategy Primary Use Case Notes
IMGT 128 (1-128) Symmetric around CDR midpoints (111/112 for CDR3) Standardized international reference Based on structural alignment; most widely used
Chothia ~113 Insertions on specific positions (31, 52, 100 for H) Structure-based analysis CDRs defined by structural loops
Kabat ~113 Insertions on specific positions (35, 52, 100 for H) Sequence-based analysis CDRs defined by sequence variability
Martin ~113 Extended Chothia; insertions at 8, 72 for H Improved Chothia scheme Additional insertion points defined
AHo 149 (1-149) Minimal insertions; most positions pre-allocated Comprehensive structural alignment Works for all Ig/TCR chains; fewest insertion codes
Wolfguy H: 101-449
L: 501-849		 Encodes chain type and region in number Engineering and computational analysis In-house scheme (Roche); no insertion codes needed

Key Differences

  • IMGT uses a fixed 128-position scheme with symmetric insertions around loop centers
  • Chothia/Kabat define CDRs differently and place insertions at specific anchor residues
  • Martin extends Chothia with additional insertion points for rare sequences
  • AHo pre-allocates maximum expected positions, minimizing need for insertion codes
  • Wolfguy encodes chain identity (H vs L) and region directly in the position number

CDR Definition Notes

  • IMGT CDRs are defined structurally based on the IMGT unique numbering
  • Chothia CDRs are based on structural loops observed in crystal structures
  • Kabat CDRs are based on sequence variability analysis
  • AHo uses the same positions for all chain types; gaps vary by chain type
  • All schemes share the same general loop locations but differ in exact boundaries
