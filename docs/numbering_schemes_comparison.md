## Scheme Characteristics

| Scheme | Total Positions | Insertion Strategy | Primary Use Case |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **IMGT** | 128 (1-128) | Symmetric around CDR midpoints | Standardized international reference |
| **Chothia** | ~113 | Insertions on specific anchor residues | Structure-based analysis |
| **Kabat** | ~113 | Insertions on specific anchor residues | Sequence-based analysis |
| **Martin** | ~113 | Extended Chothia with additional insertion points | Improved Chothia scheme |
| **AHo** | 149 (1-149) | Minimal insertions; most positions pre-allocated | Comprehensive structural alignment |
| **Wolfguy** | H: 101-449, L: 501-849 | Chain type and region encoded in number | Engineering and computational analysis |

### Key Differences

- **IMGT** uses a fixed 128-position scheme with symmetric insertions around loop centers
- **Chothia/Kabat** define CDRs differently and place insertions at specific anchor residues
- **Martin** extends Chothia with additional insertion points for rare sequences
- **AHo** pre-allocates maximum expected positions, minimizing need for insertion codes
- **Wolfguy** encodes chain identity (H vs L) and region directly in the position number

### CDR Definition Notes

- **IMGT CDRs** are defined structurally based on the IMGT unique numbering
- **Chothia CDRs** are based on structural loops observed in crystal structures
- **Kabat CDRs** are based on sequence variability analysis
- **AHo** uses the same positions for all chain types; gaps vary by chain type
- All schemes share the same general loop locations but differ in exact boundaries
